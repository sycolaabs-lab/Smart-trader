Oldest Resting
—
Kill switch. An order that rests too long is a liability, not an opportunity: the move it was placed for has already happened, and when price finally returns it is retesting a zone that is spent rather than offering the setup that was analysed. Filling it there would teach the system from a trade it would never have taken. So a resting order is cancelled after 12h, a position that never resolves is scratched after 72h, and either is killed early if price runs 1.5R further from the entry without tagging it. The clock runs on real time, so a weekend or a stalled data feed expires an order rather than freezing it. Killed trades are logged with the reason and are never counted as a win or a loss — set any field to 0 to disable that arm.

Grade and confidence are not independent. Grade is derived from confidence (A ≥70, B ≥50, C ≥30) and then downgraded a further step for a ranging market and again if similar past setups disagree. So a confidence floor below the grade's own threshold does nothing — at 45% a setup grades C and is rejected on grade regardless. The minimum grade is the knob that actually decides how much it trades.



Confidence runs structurally low. It divides by the weight of all 14 factors, including ones that are simply silent — an order block only speaks when price is inside one. So even a perfect alignment of all five timeframes reaches only ~49%, and three timeframes agreeing lands around 24%. Grade A (≥70%) needs almost the entire framework to agree at once and is genuinely rare.

That makes the bands far tighter in practice than they look: B needs roughly five timeframes plus a confirming zone, C needs about four aligned, and ordinary market conditions sit in D. To actually gather data, use the D floor — you are deliberately collecting low-conviction setups so the engine has outcomes to learn from. Cooldown stops it firing repeatedly off one piece of structure.

Correlation Engine
Dollar Index, Oil, S&P 500, VIX, and yields come from FRED (free, no rate limit). Silver and Bitcoin come from Twelve Data. Splitting sources avoids one provider's rate limit taking the whole engine down. Each instrument's influence on the score is weighted by its actual measured correlation with gold (shown in parentheses below), not an assumed relationship — a genuinely weak or unstable one (oil has historically been inconsistent) shrinks toward zero influence on its own.
Fundamental Intelligence Engine FRED — same key as above
CPI, PPI, unemployment, nonfarm payrolls, Fed funds rate, GDP, consumer sentiment, and retail sales — all real, all free via FRED. These report monthly or quarterly with real-world lag, so this reflects the latest released figures, not an NFP-day live reaction. Gold's relationship to any single print is genuinely contested among economists (e.g. rising CPI can be gold-bullish as an inflation hedge, or gold-bearish if it triggers Fed hawkishness) — these weights are a reasonable simplified lean, not settled fact.
News Sentiment Engine Alpha Vantage
Real macro/financial-market headlines with Alpha Vantage's built-in sentiment scoring — no keyword guessing. Their model scores general market tone (bullish news usually means risk-on for stocks), which is the opposite of what tends to help gold: risk-on sentiment typically reduces safe-haven demand. So this engine inverts the score before weighing it — a simplified, contested lean, not certainty, same as the Fundamental engine's caveats. Uses a separate free Alpha Vantage key (their free tier is tightly rate-limited, so this refreshes less often than the other engines).
Macro Engines correlation + fundamental + news all live
All three macro engines are connected now (above): cross-market correlation, fundamental economic data, and news sentiment. Each has its own honest caveats about interpretation — check each panel's description for what it actually measures versus what it's a simplified lean on.
Independent Audit
A separate check on the engine's work that takes none of its numbers on trust. It re-derives the price, the R:R, the stop distance against volatility and the direction implied by the factors — straight from raw candles — and compares. It also checks the data itself is intact, and whether the reasoning is citing evidence the statistics have already rejected. It reports rather than edits, with one exception: a critical finding blocks an autonomous trade, because an analysis that contradicts its own arithmetic is not a risk preference.
Accumulated Knowledge
What the system has worked out for itself, built up one day at a time and never discarded. Relationships are estimated jointly rather than one at a time, so drivers that move together split the credit instead of each claiming it. Nothing is asserted until there is enough evidence — new drivers sit in watching until they earn a claim, and anything that stops holding gets caught and downgraded rather than averaged into the past.

Noise is assumed until proven otherwise. Seven unrelated series on sixty days of data produce an in-sample fit of roughly 12% by pure chance, so in-sample R² is reported but never trusted. A relationship is only called established if it survives three separate tests: it beats randomly reshuffled data, it predicts days the model has never seen, and it holds up after correcting for the fact that several drivers were tested at once.
Market Reasoning
What the engine thinks is happening and what is driving it, assembled from everything it measured — structure across five timeframes, correlations weighted by their actual strength, macro prints, sentiment and the release calendar. No language model: every sentence traces to a number, which is what makes it checkable. Where price and its drivers disagree it says so rather than averaging the tension away.
Economic Releases refresh
NFP, CPI and FOMC routinely move gold tens of dollars in seconds. Spreads widen and stops sitting inside the noise get taken on a spike that then reverses. The engine stands aside through high-impact windows rather than trying to trade them. Dates come from FRED — the same key the correlation engine uses, no extra account needed.
Data Collected refresh
Every store the engine learns from, and how far each is from the point where it actually does something. Most of this machinery sits dormant below a threshold — the meta-labeler needs 15 labelled examples before it scores anything at all — so counting is the only way to know whether learning is happening or merely scheduled.
Paper Trading settings
Simulated only — no broker, no money, no orders leave this page. When on, every signal the engine logs opens a position on a demo account sized off your risk percentage. It runs to the signal's own take-profit or stop, and the result becomes both P&L and training data. This is what turns "62% of signals won" into "up 4.3% with an 11% drawdown" — the number that says whether the analysis is actually worth anything.
Off — no positions will be opened.
Partial take-profit. A 1:4 target on 15-minute structure can sit open for days, and an unrealised target is not a profit — the trade is exposed the whole time it waits. Banking at the halfway mark closes more trades, which is also what feeds the learning loop. Setups the engine is confident in (grade A/A+, or a meta-labeler score above the threshold) are left to run for the full target instead of clipping the winners that pay for the losers.

This changes what the engine learns, not just the P&L. Signals resolve at the level they actually exited, so a banked half-target is recorded as a ~2R win rather than 4R. That is deliberate: the record should reflect how the system actually trades, or every statistic built on it measures a strategy you are not running.

Fills are deliberately pessimistic: entries pay the spread and stops take slippage, so a nominal 1:4 winner returns slightly under 4R. A simulator that assumes perfect execution produces training data that teaches the wrong lesson. reset account

This panel does not try to predict anything. It audits the engine's own judgement over its resolved record — whether its confidence score carries real information, which sessions and regimes its edge actually lives in, and whether the setups it rejected would have made money. Background System setup
